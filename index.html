Why it exists

Before you ask

Quick questions

Do I need Suno Pro or a paid plan?
No. The tool only builds text — the Style box, lyrics metatags and Exclude Styles field you paste into Suno. It works with any Suno plan.
I don't make R&B or Neo Soul — is this still useful?
The presets are tuned for R&B, Neo Soul, Trap Soul, Latin Urban and Italian Pop Urban. The underlying protocol in the guide — hierarchy, tag rules, mix diagnostics — applies to any genre in Suno v6.
Do I need to know how to code?
No. Download the file, double-click it, it opens in your browser. No installation, no server, no account.
Is the interface only in Italian?
No — it has a one-click IT/EN switch in the top right corner. Try it in the live demo above.
Will I get future updates?
The tool is provided as-is at the version you download. Suno's own model changes over time, so treat the protocol as the durable part and the tool as a snapshot of it.
